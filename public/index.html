THE CODEX EXPERIMENT / BUILD 01

One simple game.
No simple execution.

The test is deliberately easy to understand: give an AI the rules of Snake, then judge how far it can push the finished artifact—engineering, autonomy, game feel, backend, visual identity, and proof.

01 / CONSTRAINT

Keep the premise legible

Snake is the control group: four directions, food, growth, collision. Complexity must deepen that idea without hiding it.

02 / AUTONOMY

A brain made of code

The Autopilot is deterministic software authored for this game. Its routes, risk scores, alternatives, and failures are visible; no model is hidden inside it.

03 / SYSTEMS

Depth beyond the board

Seeded runs, replay memory, generative visuals, fluid rendering, offline play, and temporary realtime rooms turn one mechanic into a connected product.

04 / RESULT

Judge the artifact

The game is the report. Play it, stress it, inspect its decisions, test its rooms, and decide what the AI behind its construction was capable of producing.
